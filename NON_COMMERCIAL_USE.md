# Non-commercial use

Trap Tracker AI tools are free to use for non-commercial conservation, research and education.

Permitted non-commercial uses include:

- conservation research;
- biodiversity monitoring by non-commercial projects;
- academic research and teaching;
- student projects;
- non-commercial ecological analysis;
- evaluation of camera trap and wildlife image datasets.

Users may modify and share the software for these purposes, provided the original Trap Tracker AI attribution, licence and citation information are retained.
